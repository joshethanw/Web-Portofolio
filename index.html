About

Bio

I've spent the last several years writing about the quiet mechanics of everyday life — labor, migration, and the small negotiations people make with the places they live. My work has appeared in regional and international outlets, and I've reported from four countries. When I'm not on assignment, I'm usually reading something too long for the subway.
